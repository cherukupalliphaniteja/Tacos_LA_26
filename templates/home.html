Since 2001

Our Story

Back in 2001, it was just us, a small stand, and one grill on Avenue 26 in Los Angeles. Nothing fancy, just a focus on making good tacos the right way.

The neighborhood started pulling up, and before we knew it, the whole block came alive with food, music, and people.

20+ years later, not much has changed. We relocated to Little Tokyo to make more room for everyone who's supported us along the way. Still cooking over an open flame, still doing things the way we always have.
